Mobile Hit Playbook 2026

What profitable mobile games build, how much they earn and how they get players, plus a deep study of Archero 2 and 20 competitors. Use the tabs to move between sections.

The short answer

The formula that keeps working

BuildOne simple, visual mechanic

Sort, jam, screw and block puzzles top the charts because one 3-second clip explains them. That makes ads cheap.

BuildOld idea, one new twist

Screw Jam (2D) lost to Screwdom (3D). Parking jam became Color Block Jam (color plus a timer). Chart leaders turn over every 12–18 months.

MoneyCharge for failure

A $4.99 "continue + time + booster" offer when you fail makes about 20% of IAP and 21–27% of first purchases in top puzzle games.

MoneyIAP and ads together

Pure ad games only work at Block Blast scale (70M daily players). Most winners hold ads back early, then sell boosters, passes and an ad-free option.

OutreachThe gameplay is the ad

About 78–90% of ad spend goes to short videos, with playables at 7–13%. If your level clip makes people want to play, installs get cheap.

MarginWeb shops for spenders

Since the April 2025 Epic v. Apple ruling, US players can be linked to a web store, which keeps about $0.92 per dollar instead of $0.70. More than 60 of the top 100 grossing games run one.

If you're a small team

A realistic version to copy

  • Pick a proven puzzle family (sort, jam, screw or conveyor) and add one twist that looks great in a clip. Pixel Flow had about 10 people and under 3 months of development.
  • Test ad videos before building the game. Run mock-up videos as cheap ads and only build the ideas that get low install costs.
  • Design the fail offer before launch. Tune difficulty spikes around a $4.99 continue, and use rewarded ads at the same moment for non-payers.
  • Soft-launch in a cheap market and only scale once day-1 retention is above roughly 40% and ad spend pays back.
  • No ad budget? Make a daily puzzle with a shareable result card (the Wordle model), or build on Roblox, where creators handle distribution.
  • Don't clone outright. A Pixel Flow copycat made $20M in weeks, then both app stores pulled it.

How it plays

You move to dodge and stand still to auto-shoot, clearing rooms one by one. After each level-up you pick a random roguelike skill. Between runs, gear, heroes and artifacts make you permanently stronger. Modes: campaign, survival, Sky Tower, auto-battle Arena PvP, the twice-daily Seal Battle boss race, and resource modes.

Why it worked
  • A large Archero 1 fanbase to win back.
  • Habby's polished template, with more ways to progress and spend.

Weaknesses

  • Pay-to-win complaints about artifacts, event currency and $30 heroes.

How competitors advertise

The 20 competitors fall into four marketing playbooks. Archero 2 sits in the first.

Playbook 1 · Paid performance at scaleHabby, Shoot the Zombies, Last War, Legend of Mushroom, Zombie Waves

Huge creative volume and power-fantasy hooks, bought on Meta, TikTok, AppLovin, Google and Chinese networks. Highest revenue, highest cost.

Playbook 2 · IP and pre-registrationSolo Leveling: Arise, Mighty DOOM, Soul Knight Prequel

A known brand fills pre-registration, then a launch-day burst. Works for launch, but doesn't fix weak retention. Mighty DOOM shut down after 17 months.

Playbook 3 · Creator-ledBrawl Stars, Bullet Echo, Survivor.io (TikTok UGC)

Creator codes with revenue share, low entry thresholds, and native-looking clips. One MrBeast video gave Brawl Stars 4.5x US installs.

Playbook 4 · Organic and platformVampire Survivors, Brotato, Magic Survival, Soul Knight, Dead Cells, Gunfire Reborn, Hades

Store featuring, PC reputation, streamers, regional publishers (Bilibili, TapTap) and subscription deals. Almost no ad spend, but low mobile revenue.

  1. The core loop is cheap to copy. The money is in the meta: gear gacha, heroes, passes. Archero 2 earns about 2.7x per player compared with Archero 1 on almost the same loop.
  2. Every paid winner advertises the same moment: a horde wiped by one huge skill. Design that moment into the first minute so a raw screen recording works as the ad.
  3. Soft-launch long in high-spending Asian markets. Archero 2 earned $31M before going global, and Zombie Waves only broke out about 15 months after launch.
  4. You're bidding against 4X games like Last War on the same shooter-style ads, and their players are worth far more. Win on efficiency (playables, real gameplay, cheap images, retargeting), not by outspending them.
  5. Cheap channels exist: Chinese mini-game funnels and static images (Shoot the Zombies), creator codes (Brawl Stars, Bullet Echo), and win-back ads aimed at fans of an earlier game.
  6. There is a trust trade-off. Fair games (Soul Knight, Vampire Survivors) get huge reach and goodwill but little money. Aggressive ones (Mighty DOOM) can collapse. Archero 2 sits in between and takes heavy pay-to-win criticism.
  7. Hits fade. Last War, Brawl Stars and Solo Leveling all fell sharply in 2025–26. Plan anniversary updates and creative refreshes, as Archero 2's anniversary recovery shows.

Before you spend big

How a soft launch works

Release the finished game in a few cheap countries, buy a small batch of players and check the numbers before going worldwide.

  1. Pick the countriesIn App Store Connect (Pricing and Availability) and Google Play Console, select only those countries. The Philippines, Malaysia and Thailand give cheap installs and widely spoken English. Canada and Australia show how US players will spend.
  2. Buy a small batch of playersSpend roughly $2K–10K on Meta, TikTok, AppLovin or Unity to get 5,000–20,000 installs. Installs cost about $0.05–0.30 in SEA versus $2–5+ in the US.
  3. MeasureRough bars for casual games: day-1 retention 40%+, day-7 15–20%, day-30 5–8%. Track with Firebase or GameAnalytics, plus AppsFlyer or Adjust for ad attribution.
  4. Fix and repeatLow day-1 retention means fixing the first 5 minutes. Low day-7 means adding depth. Low revenue means adjusting offers and ad placements. Each round takes a few weeks.
  5. Scale all at onceCapybara Go spent about two months in SEA, earning up to $800K/day, then launched a large ad push into Korea, Taiwan and the US. Archero 2's soft launch raised revenue per download about 2.7x.
