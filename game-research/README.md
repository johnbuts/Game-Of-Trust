# Game Research

Research on what makes mobile games profitable, and how they find players.

- `mobile-hit-playbook.html`: source of the research page. Live version: https://claude.ai/artifact/Xy6cuj2eN8q1vnheWAT44K

The page covers:
- the current formula for profitable mobile games
- 20 simple game ideas, each with revenue proof
- deep dives on top earners in each category
- an Archero 2 study with 20 competitors and their ad strategies
- outreach channels and a soft-launch guide

Revenue figures are third-party estimates (AppMagic, Sensor Tower and others) as of October 2026.
